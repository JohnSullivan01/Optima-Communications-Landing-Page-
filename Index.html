Questions

Common questions

How much does a camera system cost?

It depends on how many cameras you need, the size of your space, and the wiring involved. That's why we start with a free on-site assessment. You'll get exact pricing within 24 hours, with no obligation.

How long does installation take?

It depends on the size of the job. You'll get a timeline along with your pricing, and our own technicians handle the install from start to finish.

Can you work with my existing cameras or wiring?

We'll look at what you already have during the assessment and tell you honestly what can be reused and what should be upgraded.

Do I need a server or recorder on site?

Not with Verkada. Footage is stored on the cameras and in the cloud, so there's no recorder to maintain. You can watch from your phone or computer.

What areas do you serve?

Businesses across Rockland and Westchester County, NY and Bergen County, NJ.

Is there a warranty?

Yes. Installs are backed by up to a 10-year equipment warranty, and you get one local team to call for support.
